# Competitive Scan — Math Fact Fluency Apps (2025–2026)

Decision-support for **Fact Fluency** (free, browser-based, spaced-repetition +
fluency-gate, Number Munchers–style, warm/non-punitive, PWA). Sources are cited
inline. Where information is quote-gated or newly launched, uncertainty is
flagged. Snapshot taken 2026-07-12.

## 1. Per-competitor snapshots

### Direct fact-fluency competitors (closest philosophy)

**XtraMath** — Nonprofit (501c3), the incumbent "10 min/day" fact-fluency drill
for K–6.

- Explicitly built on **spaced repetition** with an adaptive algorithm that
  front-loads weak facts and reduces the frequency of mastered ones. All four
  operations.
- **Speed gate** via fixed timer tiers: 12s (added Jan 2026, for early
  learners), 6s, and 3s for "genuine automaticity." Thresholds are chosen, not
  per-kid adaptive.
- Pricing: **Free $0**; Family **$2/yr**; Teacher **$50/yr** (≤50 students);
  School **$500/yr**. Kids sign in with name + adult email + **4-digit PIN**, or
  a classroom pick-your-name page; Clever/Google SSO.
- **8 languages** incl. Spanish + ASL. Motivation historically thin (drill-like);
  added a post-session game ("Awakening") and a June 2025 Legends of Learning
  partnership. Web + apps.
- Sources: [xtramath.org](https://xtramath.org/),
  [pricing](https://home.xtramath.org/pricing),
  [class sign-in](https://home.xtramath.org/support/how-can-my-class-sign-in),
  [blog/logic](https://home.xtramath.org/blog/logic)

**Reflex Math (ExploreLearning)** — The school-market gold standard for fact
fluency, grades 2–6.

- Adaptive, individualized, **fact-family** approach; per-session fluency check +
  "just-in-time coaching"; **speed + accuracy fluency gate**. All four
  operations. Companion product **Frax** covers fractions.
- Signature **"Green Light"** daily mechanic (hit a daily correct-answer count →
  7.4× more likely to reach 100% fluency). Deep game/avatar/token rewards.
- **Strongest efficacy evidence** of any competitor: multiple i-Ready-based
  studies. A real moat for school sales.
- Pricing: **school-licensed, quote-only** (no public price, no free consumer
  tier; free teacher trial). Students log in via teacher username + class +
  password or Clever. **Requires internet** (no offline).
- Sources: [reflex.explorelearning.com](https://reflex.explorelearning.com/),
  [approach](https://info.explorelearning.com/math-fact-fluency-and-the-reflex-approach.html),
  [efficacy research](https://www.explorelearning.com/research/)

**Prodigy Math Facts** — **New** dedicated fluency mode (launched ~March 2026),
separate from the flagship Prodigy RPG.

- Clear **fluency gate**: a fact is "fluent" only after **3 correct in a row
  within the threshold (default 6s)**; ~60 questions per "race," goal one
  race/day, <10 min/day. Adaptive via customizable fact sets/thresholds; **true
  spaced-repetition schedule unconfirmed** (not documented).
- Motivation: pet **races**, unlockable rewards/collection. Teacher dashboard
  with real-time **fluency grids** (speed + accuracy).
- **Free for teachers** (1 free session/student/day). Web + app. Caveat: the
  main Prodigy game is freemium and has drawn criticism for aggressive
  membership upsell.
- Sources:
  [newsroom](https://www.prodigygame.com/main-en/prodigy-newsroom/introducing-prodigy-math-facts),
  [math-facts-practice](https://www.prodigygame.com/main-en/math-facts-practice)

**Math Facts Pro** — Small tool, but **philosophically the closest to Fact
Fluency**.

- Explicit **spaced repetition**; mastery = **9 of last 10 correct AND fast
  enough**, with **per-student customized cut-off speeds** (adaptive threshold,
  like Fact Fluency). Targets **90–95% success**; "students never see a wrong
  answer"; tracks facts-correct, not screen minutes.
- Pricing: free (100 facts + printable results); **$5/mo, $10/yr, or $20
  lifetime**. Post-session "Mars Defense" mini-game. Web. Light on gamification
  and polish.
- Source: [mathfactspro.com](https://mathfactspro.com/)

**Rocket Math (Online Tutor)** — Mastery-sequence timed drills, grades 1–12.

- 26 levels (A–Z), **4 new facts at a time** mixed with known ones (interleaving,
  not true SR), advance only at mastery (**12 in a row within a time limit** →
  speed gate). All operations + fractions/decimals.
- Pricing: **per-seat, paid** — $9 (1–19 seats) down to $3 (1,000+); 30-day
  trial. Minimal gamification. Web + app + worksheets.
- Sources: [rocketmath.com app](https://www.rocketmath.com/rocket-math-app/),
  [pricing](https://www.rocketmath.com/rocket-math-pricing/)

**Timez Attack / Big Brainz (now Imagine Math Facts)** — Immersive 3D
dungeon-adventure, mostly multiplication (+ add/sub/div added).

- Boss fights demand fast + correct answers (implicit fluency gate). **Deepest
  game-world immersion** of the group, but **aging and largely discontinued for
  consumers** — free times-tables version remains; full product folded into
  Imagine Learning's school offering. Heavy download, not browser-native.

### Multiplayer / arcade fact practice

**99math** — Free multiplayer, K–8; the social/competition play.

- Live head-to-head rounds with real-time **leaderboards**; **no student
  accounts** (join by code). Speed + accuracy are central, but it is **not
  spaced repetition and has no mastery fluency-gate**; it's competitive
  practice. All four operations + fractions/decimals/algebra.
- Free core; **99math Pro** adds analytics/topic sets/controls. Web.
- Source: [99math.com](https://www.99math.com/)

**Arcademics** — Free browser multiplayer arcade (math + ELA).

- Up to ~12 kids race on facts in real time; **no login for public rooms**. Pure
  speed/arcade — **no SR, no fluency-gate, no adaptive throttle**. Plus
  subscription adds tracking/private rooms. Web.
- Source: [arcademics.com](https://www.arcademics.com/)

**Sumdog** — Game-based math + spelling, ages 5–14.

- 39 games; **adaptive/personalized** questions; scoring by accuracy so
  mixed-level kids compete; math contests. Freemium → paid (school + family).
  Web/app.
- Source: [learn.sumdog.com](https://learn.sumdog.com/en-us/subjects/mathematics)

### General platforms with a fact-practice mode

**SplashLearn** — K–5 adaptive games.

- Dedicated Math Facts: **adaptive practice** with a **speed gate (mastered when
  answered within 6s)** + custom practice + **printable worksheets**. All four
  operations, Google Classroom integration. **Free for teachers/schools**;
  parents freemium (~$7.99–11.99/mo). Web/app.
- Source: [math-facts](https://www.splashlearn.com/math-skills/math-facts)

**Boddle** — K–6 3D adventure game.

- **Boddle Racers** multiplayer fact-fluency mode (all four ops) + skill-matched
  "Grand Prix" races; adaptive "roadmap"; curriculum-aligned; **narrated audio,
  sketch tool, video tutorials** (accessibility). Free for parents/teachers;
  Premium ~$6–10/mo (cosmetics only — same engine). Web/app.
- Source:
  [Boddle Racers](https://www.boddlelearning.com/article/boddle-racers-math-fact-fluency)

**DreamBox Math (Discovery Education)** — K–8 adaptive curriculum.

- Fluency is **embedded conceptually** (strategies, models, pattern
  recognition), **not a speed-gated fact drill**. Pricing: school ~$20/student/yr;
  home ~$12.95–19.95/mo. Web/app.
- Source: [dreambox.com](https://www.dreambox.com/)

**Zearn** — Nonprofit full K–8 curriculum, free core.

- Daily **"Number Gym"** fluency inside a CPA (concrete→pictorial→abstract)
  lesson flow; **ESSA Tier 1-rated**; classroom-oriented. Fluency is
  curriculum-embedded, **not a standalone speed gate**. Minimal gamification.
- Source: [about.zearn.org](https://about.zearn.org/how-zearn-math-works)

**Khan Academy Kids** — Ages 2–8, fully free, no ads/subscription (nonprofit).

- Adaptive leveling; add/sub + operation meaning via visuals/stories; **no
  speed/fluency gate**, conceptual and broad, not fact-fluency-specific.
- Source: [khanacademy.org/kids/math](https://www.khanacademy.org/kids/math)

## 3. Where Fact Fluency is at parity or ahead

- **True per-kid adaptive speed threshold.** Fact Fluency and Math Facts Pro are
  the only ones with _individualized_ speed cut-offs. Reflex is adaptive but
  opaque; XtraMath (3/6/12s), Prodigy (6s), SplashLearn (6s) use _fixed_
  thresholds. A genuine correctness edge.
- **Explicit ~80% success-rate throttle on new facts.** Only Math Facts Pro
  (90–95%) publicly does something equivalent. Core to the "never tedious"
  promise and defensible/marketable.
- **Fully free, no ads, no upsell.** Matches the nonprofits (XtraMath/Khan/Zearn)
  and beats the freemium crowd (SplashLearn, Boddle, Sumdog, DreamBox) and the
  paid ones (Reflex, Rocket Math). Prodigy's free tier carries a monetized parent
  funnel.
- **Works offline (PWA).** Effectively unique. Strong for spotty-connectivity
  homes/classrooms and for "install to home screen" retention.
- **Instant guest play + tap-a-profile.** Lowest-friction onboarding among
  profile-keeping tools.
- **Focused, honest scope.** SR + fluency gate done well, without the grind of
  Rocket/XtraMath drills or the RPG-monetization drift of Prodigy. The Number
  Munchers mechanic is distinctive and carries parent nostalgia — no direct
  competitor uses a munch-the-grid mechanic.

## 4. Gaps / where competitors are ahead

- **Multiplayer / social.** Biggest gap. 99math, Arcademics, Boddle Racers,
  Prodigy, Sumdog all lean on live or matched competition; we have none.
- **Efficacy research / credibility.** Reflex (multiple i-Ready studies),
  DreamBox, and Zearn (ESSA Tier 1) publish evidence. We have zero published
  outcomes — a hard blocker for school procurement.
- **Classroom/roster + SSO.** XtraMath, Reflex, SplashLearn, Prodigy support
  Clever/ClassLink/Google Classroom rostering.
- **Curriculum-standards alignment.** DreamBox, Zearn, SplashLearn, Boddle market
  Common Core alignment; Zearn/DreamBox tout ESSA ratings. We don't map to
  standards (e.g., 2.OA.2, 3.OA.7).
- **Localization / accessibility.** XtraMath ships 8 languages + ASL; Boddle has
  narrated audio; we don't mention Spanish, screen-reader/narration,
  dyslexia-friendly fonts, or dyscalculia accommodations.
- **Printable reports / worksheets.** XtraMath (premium), SplashLearn, Rocket
  Math, Math Facts Pro all export printables.
- **Strategy teaching / conceptual scaffolding.** Reflex's "just-in-time
  coaching," DreamBox's CPA, Boddle's video tutorials teach _how_ to derive a
  fact. We only practice recall; a missed fact gets no strategy support.
- **Depth of game world & native apps.** Prodigy, Boddle, and (historically)
  Timez Attack offer immersive worlds and store presence; a single mechanic +
  PWA is lighter and risks novelty fatigue over months.

## 5. Top differentiation opportunities (ranked)

Filtered to fit the product's philosophy (short, non-tedious, fluency-first,
warm) and to be _better than_, not just match, the field.

1. **Non-punitive async multiplayer** ("ghost race" vs. your own best, or
   sibling/family async challenges) — closes the #1 gap without importing
   99math's stressful live leaderboards. **Effort: M**
2. **Weekly parent recap as an emailed/printable PDF + trickiest-facts
   worksheet** — cheap, high perceived value, offline-friendly. **Effort: S**
3. **Strategy hint on a miss** (show the near-fact / double / count-on /
   make-ten), not just right/wrong — Reflex-style coaching, lightweight and warm.
   **Effort: M**
4. **Make the per-kid adaptive speed threshold a headline, explained feature**
   (with a visible "getting faster" trend) — turn an engine strength into a
   marketing wedge. **Effort: S**
5. **Spanish localization + narrated audio for pre/emerging readers** — XtraMath
   proves demand; audio also serves accessibility and the K–1 market. **Effort:
   M**
6. **Publish a transparent methodology + efficacy page; add opt-in anonymized
   outcome tracking to seed a real study** — attacks the credibility gap vs.
   Reflex/Zearn. **Effort: M writeup / L for a study**
7. **Gentle accessibility/dyscalculia mode** (no-timer "confidence" setting,
   reduced-motion, dyslexia-friendly font, high-contrast, subitizing visuals) —
   inclusive, on-brand, unmatched by the arcade competitors. **Effort: M**
8. **Standards mapping surfaced in the dashboard** (CCSS 2.OA.2 / 3.OA.7, etc.) —
   mostly labeling; lowers teacher-adoption friction. **Effort: S**
9. **Lightweight classroom mode** (class-code join + optional Clever/Google SSO +
   simple roster) — opens the school channel; keep it optional so home UX stays
   login-free. **Effort: L**
10. **Multiple game skins over the same engine** (rotate mini-games beyond
    munch-the-grid) — counters single-mechanic fatigue; reuses the SR/fluency
    core. **Effort: M**
11. **Calm reward meta** (collectible muncher "sticker book," milestone-linked
    themes, Duolingo-style streak-freeze) — extends motivation without
    punishment. **Effort: S–M**
12. **Marketed anti-grind guarantee** (parent-set daily goal + a hard "done for
    today" stop) — turns a core principle into a claim no competitor makes.
    **Effort: S**

## Honest uncertainties

- **Prodigy Math Facts** is brand-new (~March 2026); its speed gate (3×@6s) is
  documented but its _spaced-repetition_ schedule is not — treat its SR as
  unconfirmed.
- **Reflex** pricing is quote-only; the per-student cost isn't public. No
  evidence Reflex is being retired — it's actively marketed alongside Frax.
- Vendor use of "adaptive" is often marketing; for DreamBox/Zearn/Sumdog/Boddle a
  _true_ box-based SR scheduler could not be confirmed — they appear to be
  difficulty-adaptive, which differs from Leitner-style due-scheduling.
- SplashLearn offline behavior via its native app is unclear; treated as
  online-first.
